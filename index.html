Why night rail

You travel while the world sleeps.

A berth costs less than a hotel and a night. Mornings arrive with a view you did not have to drive to.

Time

Give the day back to the day

Leave after work and skip the airport queue, the security line and the seat you cannot sleep in. The distance is covered while you are offline.

Room

A bed, a table, a window

Your compartment is yours for the night. Fold down the berth, put your bag on the rack and keep the light to yourself.

Arrival

Step out in the centre

Trains stop where people live. You get off in the middle of the city with the whole morning ahead of you.
